# Forest / editorial portfolio

This is an artwork-led experience. The uploaded rework establishes its visual identity: a full-viewport forest animation, warm white type, charcoal and forest surfaces, and restrained chartreuse. Preserve this direction while making the artwork and controls easier to use.

Use local Manrope for the interface and italic serif emphasis in display headings. Keep asymmetric project proportions on desktop and a single column on phones. Preserve complete artwork rather than cropping away the composition. Numbered sections and projects follow the supplied brief. Give content generous spacing and controls clear focus indicators and touch targets.

Palette: background #111512, panel #1a201b, text #f0f0e7, secondary #a0a89e, accent #d5ea92. Avoid lowering opacity on small text. Icons are authored, consistent-stroke SVG symbols in icons.svg rather than font-dependent glyphs.

The forest video is the main motion moment. Image hover responses are brief and limited to fine pointers. Keyboard navigation skips decorative transitions. Reduced-motion visitors get a poster, with animation disabled; other visitors can pause motion and retain that choice. Project video uses native controls and stops when its viewer closes.

Design guidance used:
- [Impeccable](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable), skill version 4.7.1: layout, typography, responsive states and visual/accessibility checks. Its context engine was unavailable; project context was read directly.
- [Emil Kowalski: design engineering](https://github.com/emilkowalski/skills/blob/main/skills/emil-design-eng/SKILL.md): restrained motion, interruption, reduced motion, touch hover gating and interaction feedback.

The user's supplied art direction and content take precedence over generic style preferences in either resource.
