# Zakariae Bekri portfolio

A personal portfolio for a Morocco-based visual designer working across 3D modeling, product visualization, graphic design and digital art. Visitors should see the artwork, understand the artist's contribution, and contact him directly.

The uploaded portfolio and rework plan supply the content and design direction. Preserve all 17 projects. Lead with 3D work; keep design, illustration and web/UI available through filters. Do not invent client relationships, awards or career claims. Profile and contact details come from data/site.json.

GitHub Pages serves the repository root without a build. JSON content and local media must remain editable through Pages CMS. The owner's GitHub App authorization and first authenticated CMS save are separate from local validation. No backend, contact form service or analytics is required.

Shared project URLs use hash routes. The project viewer must support keyboard closing, focus restoration, optional gallery/video and copying links. Respect reduced motion and allow visitors to pause the hero. Keep original compiled assets for reference. Prepare the rework on a review branch; merging and publication remain separate actions.
