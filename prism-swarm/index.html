Game dev · My own game · OpenGame Jam #1

A fast neon arcade game. A swarm of glowing shapes comes at you in red, green and blue. Match their color to fight back, and survive as long as you can.

▶  Play free in your browser Scroll to explore. Turn the sound on for game music. Tap the colored dots up top to change your color, then click the shapes to pop the ones that match.

How it plays

One simple rule.

You are a little prism of light, and you can switch your color any time. Everything else follows from that.

  • Your shots hit your colorBe red to hurt red monsters. Switch to hit the others.
  • Your color can't hurt youMonsters of your color pass right through you. Swap to slip out of a trap.
  • Catch your color to healBullets that match you give health back instead of taking it.

Read the screen

Everything you need, at a glance.

  1. YouThe glowing prism in the middle. Your shots fly out on their own.
  2. Combo and Spectrum BurstDefeat all three colors in 3 seconds and a white wave clears the screen.
  3. Danger meterGoes from “Vibing” to total panic as the swarm grows.
  4. Your colorTap 1, 2 or 3 to become red, green or blue.
  5. DashA quick burst of speed to escape a tight spot.

Level up

Grab gems. Pick a power.

Every level gives you three cards to choose from. The powers have silly names on purpose, because a good laugh makes a hard run more fun.

Common

Zoomies

Move faster. Gotta go fast-ish.

Common

Balls of Steel

Glowing balls circle you and hit every color.

Rare

Emotional Damage

More damage. They'll need therapy.

Evolution

White Prism

Mix the right powers and every shot hits every color.

Every 5 minutes

Big, flashy bosses.

A countdown warns you, then a boss arrives: Mortar Star, The Lighthouse, The Hydra, The Splitter or The Eclipse. Their attacks are bright and loud, but you can always see them coming and dodge.

The fifth boss is a powered up surprise called the Final Exam.

Heavy juice

Light, sound and shake.

Glow, screen shake, combo counters, pop sounds and a hype announcer. Every hit is meant to feel good.

Behind the game

Made almost only with code.

The shapes, glow and effects are all drawn by code in Godot 4. The sound effects and music are generated by a small program too, not recorded. The only hand-made pictures are the logo and the menu background.

  • One idea that grows everything: three colors
  • Plays on PC, in the browser and on phones
  • Worldwide high scores for each mode
